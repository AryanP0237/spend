        Privacy and backup

        Your spending is stored only on this phone. It is never sent anywhere and nobody else can see it. But if you lose your phone or delete the app, it’s gone, so save a backup now and then.

        “Save backup file” opens the share menu: choose Save to Files (iCloud Drive). To restore, tap “Restore from backup” and pick that file.
